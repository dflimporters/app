Getting started

One app, one login, works on your phone browser — no app store needed.

  1. 1Go to dflhq.com on your phone or computer.
  2. 2Sign in one of two ways: Microsoft sign-in if you have a DFL email, or phone number + text code for everyone else.Enter your number → you'll get an SMS code → type it in → you're in.
  3. 3You'll land on My Numbers, your home screen, with five tabs along the bottom.

My Numbers

Your home tab — how you're tracking against target this month, at a glance.

The top card: are you on pace?

Shows your month-to-date sales against your target, with a percentage and a progress bar. A small tick mark on the bar shows where you should be based on how many selling days have passed — the text next to it says something like "Should be at 62%".

On or ahead of pace A little behind Well behind — needs attention

My Orders

Total orders and value for the month, then five tiles: Open, Shipping, On Hold, Back Order, Credit Notes. Tap any tile with a number on it to see that list — toggle between biggest-value-first and oldest-first.

Biggest gaps to close

Your three most-behind customers, ready to tap straight into their pre-call brief. Tap View all → to see everyone in the Customers tab.

TipCheck the "Updated" line at the bottom of the screen to see how fresh the numbers are, and pull down on the page to refresh.

Customers

Search any account, or work down a prioritized list.

Three ways to sort

  • Biggest gap — furthest below their monthly budget first.
  • Top sales — your best accounts this month.
  • No order yet — budgeted accounts with $0 in sales so far — treat this as your call list.
Watch outAn account with no budget set won't show a gap — it just says no budget instead of a dollar figure. That's not a bug.

  • Toggle YTD / MTD to switch the whole card between year-to-date and this month.
  • Category gaps — categories they haven't bought from in the longest.
  • Replenishment due and Key SKU opportunities — items overdue or never bought. This is your talking list for the visit.
04

Weekly Plan

Submit every Friday for the coming week — 4 short steps.

  1. 1Who Are You? — pick your name and which week (This week / Next week / Week after).
  2. 2This Week's Commitment — type your $ sales goal.
  3. 3Day-by-Day Coverage — for each day, pick your zones and key stores from a searchable list. Can't find one? Use + Add a zone/store not on the list.
  4. 4Anything to Flag — optional notes on pricing issues, competitor activity, etc.
Watch outSubmit stays disabled until your name is picked and every day has at least one zone — missing fields outline in red.

Submitting again for the same week just updates your plan — it won't create a duplicate.

After you submit

  • Generate Store Briefs — makes downloadable PDF pre-call briefs per day/store.
  • Share on WhatsApp and Download (image of your summary).
  • Submit Another to log a different week.
Watch outNo signal when you hit submit? The button turns red and says Failed — tap to retry. Just tap it again once you're back online.

FAQ

Common questions

My numbers look stale — is something broken?

Check the "Updated" timestamp at the bottom of My Numbers, then pull down on the page to refresh. Data only updates when the page loads.

I can't find a customer

Double-check the spelling or try their customer ID. If it's still not there, that account likely isn't assigned to you — check with your sales manager.

Why won't Submit light up on the Weekly Plan?

You're missing your name, or a day with no zone selected — look for anything outlined in red.

I never got my SMS code

Double-check the number you entered and wait a minute — delivery can lag. If it still doesn't arrive, let your manager or the DFL admin team know; it's usually a carrier issue, not something wrong with your device.

Where's the sign-out button?

Tap your initials in the top-right circle, then Sign out. It logs you out immediately with no confirmation, so make sure that's what you meant to do.

Need help beyond this guide? Ask your sales manager, or the DFL admin team for login and account issues.

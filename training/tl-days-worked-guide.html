Days Worked & Payroll

See which days your team worked, and submit relief days and extra days for pay every Wednesday.

What it is Opening it Reading the screen The rules Submitting After you submit Questions
00

What it is

Days Worked & Payroll shows which days each person on your team worked, and is where you submit relief days and extra days for pay every Wednesday.

  • Who you see: your own team, plus any temporary relief worker who covered one of your stores. Nobody from other teams.
  • A day worked = the person logged at least one store visit that day. No visit logged means no day worked, so make sure your team logs every visit.
  • History: this pay fortnight and the two before it (about one month).
  • Where the pay goes: your submission goes straight into the Relief Coverage log. Admin pays it from there in the next pay run. You do not fill in Relief Coverage yourself.

02

Reading the screen

Each person has a card with one box per day of the fortnight: Monday to Sunday, twice.

  • Fortnight buttons at the top switch between this fortnight and the two before it.
  • Totals: how many Brand Ambassadors, extra days so far, and temporary relief days.
  • Card numbers: "8/10 worked" means 8 days worked out of 10 owed. An amber +1 extra badge means they are over. Red text such as "2 weekdays not logged" means weekdays with no visit.
  • Sections: Brand Ambassadors, then Permanent relief, then Temporary relief.

03

The rules

Everyone except temporary relief is owed 10 paid weekdays per fortnight; only days over that are extra.

  • Pay fortnights run Monday to Sunday, two weeks at a time, starting Monday 2 November 2026 (2–15 Nov, 16–29 Nov, 30 Nov–13 Dec, and so on).
  • Holidays and leave count toward the 10. A public holiday or a day of approved leave reduces the days owed. A fortnight with Heroes Day owes 9 days.
  • Swaps are not extra. Taking Wednesday off and working Saturday is still 10 days, so nothing extra is due.
  • Working a public holiday is extra.
  • The rest of the week is assumed worked. You submit on Wednesday, so today and the remaining weekdays count as worked (dashed boxes).

04

Submitting every Wednesday

Use the Submit relief & extra days box at the top of the screen.

1

Check the week

The dropdown should say This week. Pick Last week only if you missed a Wednesday.

2

Check who is listed

The app fills in a line for everyone with days to pay:

  • Temporary relief: the days they logged this week are already ticked.
  • Brand Ambassadors and permanent relief: only people over their 10 days, with their extra days ticked (weekend and holiday days first).
3

Tick the right days

Tap a day to add or remove it. You can tick Thursday or Friday ahead of time. A small green dot under a day means a visit was logged. Greyed-out, crossed-out days are already in Relief Coverage and can't be added again.

4

Choose a reason Relief

Vacation Leave, Sick Leave, Emergency Leave, Backfill for resignation, Training, Absent or Other. Extra-day lines always say "Extra day worked".

5

Covering for Optional

For temporary relief, type who was off.

6

Check the rate per day

It's filled in from their usual rate. Change it only if this week is different.

7

Remove or add people

Remove anyone who shouldn't be paid with the × on their line. Add someone missing with + Add someone….

8

Add a note Optional

Use Note for payroll if something needs explaining.

9

Check the total and submit

Check the days and J$ total at the bottom, tap Submit, then confirm.

WatchThe app won't let you submit a relief line without a reason, or any line without a rate.
05

After you submit

A green message confirms how many days went to Relief Coverage, and those days get a blue dot on each person's card.

  • Relief Coverage: each ticked day is now an entry in the Relief Coverage log, with you as supervisor, waiting for the pay run. Admin creates the pay run; you do nothing more.
  • Your recent submissions (bottom of the screen) lists each week you sent: who, which dates, the reason and the J$ amount.
  • Skipped days: if a day was already in Relief Coverage (for example logged by hand), the message says it was skipped. It is never paid twice.
  • Made a mistake? Change the days, reasons or rates and tap Resubmit. This replaces that week's submission, as long as admin hasn't paid it yet.
  • Already paid? Paid days can't be changed here. Tell admin.
06

Common questions

Someone worked but shows a red day

They didn't log a visit that day. Ask them to log visits every day; a day with no visit doesn't count.

A relief worker who covered my stores is missing

They appear once they log a visit at one of your stores. If not, use + Add someone… or tell admin.

Someone on my team is missing

They aren't set up under you as team leader. Tell admin.

I missed Wednesday

Submit anyway: pick Last week in the week dropdown.

Someone takes a day off after I submit

Change their days and tap Resubmit before the pay run.

A day is greyed out and crossed out

It's already in Relief Coverage (submitted before, or logged by hand).

The extra days look wrong

Check the card for unlogged days, holidays and approved leave. You can change the ticked days before submitting.

Admin opened my app and saw a greyed-out Submit

Admin can view your screen but can't submit for you. Only you can.

Something else is wrong

Tell your manager or admin.

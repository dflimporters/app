Getting started

Sign in with your phone — no separate app to install first.

Go to dflhq.com, enter your phone number, and type in the SMS code you receive. First time signing in? Your account is created automatically — a team leader or admin may need to approve it before you see your stores.

02

Logging a store visit

Four stages, always in this order: Log visit → Stock → Pictures → Close out. You can leave and come back — reopening a visit resumes exactly where you stopped.

1

Log visit arrival

Search and select your store from your assigned list, then tap Log visit — I've arrived →. This captures your GPS location and the time.

Don't see your store? It isn't assigned to you yet — tell your team leader.

2

Stock optional

A grid of about 13 product categories. Tap a tile to cycle it: off → stocked (green) → out of stock → off. This is a fast shelf check, separate from the full stock count tool.

Want exact quantities instead? Tap the "Stock count" row to open the full tool — see Stock features. Either way, Next: Pictures → always works.

3

Pictures 3–5 photos

Opens your phone's camera directly — you can't pick from your gallery, so old photos won't work. Take at least 3 shelf photos (up to 5); each shows a ✓ Saved once uploaded. Tap the × on a thumbnail to retake one.

Next: Close out → stays greyed out until you have 3.

4

Close out departure

Choose an outcome — ✓ All good, ⚠ Issues, or ↩ Follow-up — add optional notes (typed or spoken, see Voice notes), then tap Close out — leaving store →. This is the step that actually finishes the visit.

Watch outBest practice: take your photos on the way out, once the shelf reflects the visit you just did.
03

The three "stock" features

Three different tools share the word "stock" — here's how to tell them apart.

FeatureWhere you find itWhat it's forRequired?
Category tiles Stage 2 of a visit Fast shelf-presence check, by category Optional
Stock Count tool Stage 2's "Stock count" row, Home's "My stock counts", or My Week Exact quantity per SKU, with a suggested reorder Optional, any time that day
Report OOS Stock tab Flag specific named products as out-of-stock or damaged Standalone — no visit needed

Using the Stock Count tool

Opens a sheet of that store's real SKUs, grouped into category tabs with a search box. Each line shows the last purchase date, quantity, and average, plus an urgency color, and two boxes: Stock in storeroom and an auto-suggested reorder quantity you can adjust. You don't have to fill in every SKU — save covers whatever you counted.

04

Voice notes

Tap the 🎤 on any Notes field to speak instead of type — it turns red/⏹ while listening and transcribes live. The result is labeled "Voice transcript — RAW": it's your exact words, not an AI-cleaned summary — that part of the feature isn't switched on yet. Tap Use this to keep it, or Dismiss to throw it away and try again.

05

Offline & GPS

  • No signal when you tap "I've arrived"? The visit queues on your phone with an amber ⏳ N unsynced badge, and syncs automatically once you're back online — or tap it to sync manually.
  • GPS failing to lock doesn't stop you from logging a visit — it just proceeds without coordinates.
  • Store search only shows stores assigned to you. If one's missing, that's a roster issue, not a search problem — flag it to your team leader.

06

For team leaders

You land on a single dashboard instead of the tab bar: team stats, a Store Audit tool (rate shelf cleanliness, presence, facing, rotation, pricing and POS on a 1–5 star scale — it rolls up into an overall %), a Today's activity feed with a Today / This week toggle showing each merchandiser's progress trail (📍 arrival → 📦 Stock → 📷 Photos → 🚪 Close out) plus who hasn't started yet, and a roster of progress cards.

Viewing a merchandiser's app

Tap anyone on your roster to open their app exactly as they see it — a yellow "Viewing as [Name] · read-only" bar appears, and every Log/Submit/Close-out button is disabled. Nothing you do there gets recorded under their name — it's for coaching, not editing. Tap Exit to return to your own dashboard.

FAQ

Common questions

Why can't I find my store?

Store lists are assignment-based — search only shows stores on your route. If it's missing, ask your team leader to check your assignments.

"Next: Close out" won't light up

You need at least 3 shelf photos taken with the in-app camera before you can move on.

My visit shows "unsynced" — did it save?

Yes — it's queued on your device and will sync automatically once you're back online. You can also tap it to sync right away.

Do I need to fill in Stock (Stage 2) to finish a visit?

No — Stock is optional. Pictures (Stage 3) is the only stage that's required before you can close out.

I didn't get my SMS code

Double-check your number and wait a minute for delivery. Still nothing? Tell your team leader or an admin — it's usually a carrier issue, not your device.

Need help beyond this guide? Ask your team leader — or an admin for sign-in and account access issues.

What's expected

These are the core responsibilities of every Brand Ambassador, in every store.

Coverage

Be at every assigned store, every week

  • Visit 100% of assigned stores each week, on each store's service day.
  • Log the first visit of the day before 9am. The Coordinator follows up with anyone who has no activity by then.
Store visit

Complete every visit in the app, start to finish

  1. Log visitTap on arrival. Records the time and GPS location.
  2. StockMark each category as stocked or out of stock.
  3. Photos3 to 5, camera only, taken in store once the shelf is fixed.
  4. Close outSet the outcome, report bad goods with a photo, add notes.

A visit is not finished until it is closed out.

Shelf

Keep the shelf to standard

  • Set as displayed on the planogram, or as instructed by the Team Leader
  • DFL products well spaced and dominant
  • Every product fully faced and pulled to the front
  • First in, first out: older stock at the front
  • Correct shelf prices, with shelf talkers in place
  • All POS (danglers, wobblers, shelf strips, promo material) up and current where applicable or allowed
  • Shelves free of dust, damage and debris
Replenishment

Own every store's replenishment order

Replenishment orders are one of the most important parts of the role. The Brand Ambassador is responsible for making sure every store orders the right products, in the right quantities, on time.

  • Count storeroom stock after restocking the shelf.
  • Check each SKU's purchase history and build the reorder.
  • Push for the best order possible: full range, enough depth to last until the next visit, and any new or promoted lines.
  • Get the order to the store and the Account Manager, and follow up until it is placed.
  • Watch the "Not Buying" list for key SKUs not bought in 45+ days, and get them back on order.

The standard: no out-of-stocks caused by a missed, late or short order.

Relationships

Build strong relationships with the key people in each store

Good relationships give the Brand Ambassador the leverage to secure the best order, the best shelf space and the support to keep DFL products in front of shoppers.

  • Know the key stakeholders in every store by name: store manager, buyer or purchasing officer, receiving, and department or shelf supervisors.
  • Be professional, dependable and visible on every visit, so they trust your recommendations.
  • Use those relationships to agree orders, win extra space and displays, and get POS put up.
  • Raise store concerns with the Account Manager early, before they become problems.
Reporting

Be the rep's eyes in the store

  • Report out-of-stocks, bad goods, competitor activity and pricing errors.
  • Make sure each flagged issue is resolved within 48 hours.
Sales

Grow sales in every store

Know each store's target and actual in My Stores, and work to push every store past 100%.

Communication

Stay reachable and keep everyone informed

  • Answer calls, texts and WhatsApp messages from your Team Leader and Account Managers promptly during working hours.
  • Never be uncontactable for more than 24 hours without a justified reason.
  • Tell your Team Leader straight away if you can't make a visit, are running late, or have a phone or transport problem.
  • Keep Account Managers updated on orders, stock and store issues.
  • Request leave through the app so coverage can be arranged.

How commission is earned

Commission is paid per store, per month, against that store's sales target. It is visible live in the app.

Store resultCommission for that store
Below 80%Nothing
80–99%0.25% of the store's sales
100% or more0.5% of target, plus 0.75% of sales above target. Roughly three times the 80% tier.

Shared stores pay half. Route-only stores do not earn commission.

The ideal Brand Ambassador

These are the qualities that set the best Brand Ambassadors apart.

Reliable

Logs the first visit well before 9am, visits every store on its service day, and never leaves a visit open.

Owns the shelf

Every store follows the planogram or the Team Leader's instructions. Shelves are fully faced, rotated, priced and clean, all allowed POS is in place, and the photos prove it.

Owns replenishment

Counts stock and secures the best order possible before anything runs out. Chases SKUs the store has stopped buying.

Builds relationships

Is known and trusted by the store manager, buyer and staff, and uses that trust to win bigger orders, better space and displays.

Thinks commercially

Knows each store's target and actual, and which stores are at risk. Pushes stores past 100%.

An early warning system

Flags problems before the rep has to ask. Notes are specific and useful.

Honest data

GPS matches the store, photos are real and current, and visit times reflect the actual work done.

Communicative

Always easy to reach and quick to respond. Tells the Team Leader about problems before being asked, keeps Account Managers informed, and steps in to cover for teammates.

Keeps improving

Builds knowledge of the categories and the industry, and brings ideas to the team.

Quarterly evaluation

Each criterion is scored 1–5, for a total out of 60. The evaluation must be signed off by the Brand Ambassador and a supervisor or manager.

  1. Monitoring stock & replenishment orders (stock counts, reorders, stock report to reps)
  2. Attendance and punctuality
  3. Collaborates with sales reps and team members
  4. Rotation of goods
  5. Creative and innovative thinking
  6. Ability to analyse data and draw conclusions
  7. Time management: planning and prioritising
  8. Knowledge of industry trends and issues
  9. Displaying of goods on shelves (per planogram or as instructed by TL; POS where allowed)
  10. Proactive communication (calls, texts, emails answered on time)
  11. Shelf maintenance (dusting and wiping goods)
  12. Attentive to customer needs, makes product recommendations
12–44 Unsatisfactory 45–59 Satisfactory 60 Exceptional

When standards are not met

Warnings are documented and escalate in this order.

  1. Warning 1Formal verbal warning, documented.
  2. Warning 2Written warning. A performance improvement plan is agreed.
  3. Warning 3Formal review, scheduled within 5 working days.

Reasons for a warning include: missed store visits without reason; poor or missing photos; not flagging out-of-stocks; missed or late replenishment orders; shelves not set to the planogram or the TL's instruction; GPS showing the Brand Ambassador was not at the store; not communicating or not responding to the Team Leader or Account Managers; not contactable for more than 24 hours without a justified reason.
